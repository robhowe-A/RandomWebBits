TRY: Determine WiFi version

Wi-Fi 6/6E is the most recent standard of wireless communication. It is capable of up to 9.6 Gbit/s bandwidth. Commonly, Wi-Fi networks have visible SSID so you and others can connect your devices.

Household routers are often a combination of an Access Point (AP) and a DHCP server.

Packets are sent using varying radio channels on 2.4/5/6 GHz ranges.

Common channels combinations are 1, 6, 11 of 2.4GHz networks

5GHz channels include 36, 40, 44, and 48; each channel is separated by 5GHz
